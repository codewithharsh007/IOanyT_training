> "We need a feature where employees can submit expense reports. They upload receipts, categorize expenses, and submit for manager approval. Managers should see a queue and approve/reject with comments. Finance team needs a monthly summary report."

Using Claude, produce:

1. **Project Brief** (1 page)

   - Scope (in/out)
   - Success metrics
   - Timeline estimate
   - Key risks

2. **Epic Breakdown** (list of 3-5 epics with descriptions)

3. **Sprint Plan** (what goes in Sprint 1 vs Sprint 2)

## Deliverable
Push `pm-output.md` to your branch.
