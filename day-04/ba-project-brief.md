# BA Simulation — E-Commerce Feature Brief

## Project: Enhanced Shopping Cart for GreenGrocers

After the messy email exercise, your team now takes on the full BA role. Using the requirements you extracted (and assuming you've clarified the major contradictions), produce a complete BA package.

## Deliverables (150 minutes)

1. **Product Requirements Document (PRD)** — Use `prd-template.md`
2. **User Flow Diagram** — Visual flow from "Add to Cart" through "Order Confirmed"
3. **User Stories with Acceptance Criteria** — Use `user-story-template.md`
4. **Risk & Assumption Log** — Use `risk-assumption-log.md`

## Presentation (10 min + 5 min Q&A)
Present your BA package to the evaluators. Be ready for probing questions:
- "Why did you prioritize X over Y?"
- "What happens if the Singapore launch is moved up?"
- "How confident are you in this timeline?"

## Use AI For
- Generating user flow diagrams (Mermaid syntax)
- Drafting user stories from requirements
- Identifying edge cases you might miss

## Don't Let AI Do
- Make prioritization decisions (that's YOUR judgment)
- Skip contradictions (address them, don't ignore them)
