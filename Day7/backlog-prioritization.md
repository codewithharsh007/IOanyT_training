# Backlog Prioritization Exercise

## Context
You are the PM for "HealthTrack", a fitness tracking app. You have a 2-week sprint (10 working days, 2 developers). Each developer can deliver ~15 story points per sprint.

**Total capacity: 30 story points**

## Feature Backlog (15 Items)

Score each using RICE, then select what fits in the sprint.

| # | Feature | Estimated Effort (SP) | Notes |
|---|---------|----------------------|-------|
| 1 | Social sharing (share workouts to social media) | 5 | Marketing team requested |
| 2 | Fix crash on Samsung Galaxy S22 | 2 | 8% of users affected |
| 3 | Apple Watch integration | 13 | Top user request, complex |
| 4 | Dark mode | 5 | #2 most requested feature |
| 5 | Push notification for workout reminders | 3 | Engagement team says critical |
| 6 | Export data as CSV | 2 | 3 enterprise customers asked |
| 7 | In-app purchase for premium features | 8 | Revenue team priority |
| 8 | Fix timezone bug in streak counter | 1 | Users in Asia losing streaks |
| 9 | Onboarding tutorial redesign | 5 | Activation rate is 30% (low) |
| 10 | Add yoga and stretching category | 3 | Growing user segment |
| 11 | Performance optimization (app launch time) | 3 | Currently 4.2s, target <2s |
| 12 | Admin dashboard for support team | 8 | Support team using raw SQL |
| 13 | Offline mode (sync when back online) | 8 | Gym users lose data underground |
| 14 | Add heart rate zone training | 5 | Competitive feature |
| 15 | Fix notification permission dialog | 1 | 60% of users deny due to bad UX |

## Deliverable

1. RICE scores for all 15 items (use the RICE Scoring Guide)
2. Your sprint selection (≤30 SP, prioritized)
3. What you'd cut and why
4. One paragraph: "If the CEO asks why Feature X isn't in this sprint, here's what I'd say"

## Time: 60 minutes
