# PM Under Pressure — Scenario Brief

## The Situation

You're the PM at IOanyT. A client ("CloudMetrics") has requested 5 features for their monitoring dashboard. The deadline is 2 weeks from today. Budget is fixed (no more money). Your team: 2 developers.

## The 5 Features Requested

### Feature A: Real-Time Alert Dashboard
- Show active alerts with severity, timestamp, affected service
- Filter by severity (Critical, Warning, Info)
- One-click acknowledge
- Estimated effort: 3-5 days for 1 developer

### Feature B: Historical Trend Charts
- Line charts for CPU, memory, disk over time (1h, 24h, 7d, 30d)
- Zoom and pan on charts
- Export chart as PNG
- Estimated effort: 4-6 days for 1 developer

### Feature C: Team-Based Access Control
- Multiple teams, each sees only their services
- Admin can assign services to teams
- Role-based permissions (viewer, editor, admin)
- Estimated effort: 5-8 days for 1 developer

### Feature D: Email + Slack Notifications
- Alert notifications via email and Slack
- Configurable thresholds per service
- Notification preferences per user
- Estimated effort: 2-3 days for 1 developer

### Feature E: Custom Report Builder
- Drag-and-drop report builder
- Schedule reports (daily, weekly, monthly)
- Export as PDF
- Estimated effort: 6-10 days for 1 developer

## Client's Email

> "We need ALL five features by the 15th. Our board meeting is on the 16th and I'm presenting the new dashboard. This is non-negotiable."

## Your Task (120 min)

1. **Estimate** each feature using Claude (be specific — break into subtasks)
2. **Create a delivery plan**: What's in? What's out? What's at risk?
3. **Prepare a 5-minute presentation** defending your plan to the client
4. **Be ready for tough questions**:
   - "Why can't your developers just work overtime?"
   - "Feature C is the most important to us — why did you deprioritize it?"
   - "Our competitor delivered all this in one week"
