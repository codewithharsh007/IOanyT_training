# Process Mapping Exercise — Client Brief

## The Project

**Client**: FoodieReview (fictional)
**Industry**: Restaurant review platform
**Request**: Build a customer feedback portal for a chain of 25 restaurants

## Background

FoodieReview operates 25 restaurants across 3 cities. Currently, customer feedback is collected via:
- Paper comment cards (rarely filled out)
- Google Reviews (no control, often unfair)
- Occasional email complaints

The owner wants a **digital feedback portal** where:
1. Customers can submit feedback after dining (via QR code on receipt)
2. Restaurant managers can see real-time feedback for their location
3. The owner can see trends across all 25 locations
4. Negative feedback triggers an immediate alert to the manager

## Your Task

Map out the **complete software delivery process** for this project. Cover all SDLC phases from requirements gathering to deployment and maintenance.

## Deliverable
- Process map (diagram or structured document)
- For each phase: who does what, what tools, what outputs
- Identify 3+ risks and how you'd mitigate them
- 10-minute team presentation

## Deliberately Vague Areas (Navigate These)
- Budget isn't specified
- Mobile app or web app? (They didn't say)
- "Real-time" — what does that mean exactly?
- Multi-language support? (3 cities might mean different languages)
